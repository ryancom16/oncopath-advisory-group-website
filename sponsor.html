Program Model

A focused alternative to conference-scale engagement.

The Fellows Exchange format is intentionally compact: a private-room dinner, one educational theme, physician-led moderation, and a carefully invited fellow audience.

The goal is a credible learning environment for fellows and a professionally managed sponsorship channel for organizations that support oncology education.

Event Presence

A clear role before anyone enters the room.

Sponsor presence may include a reviewed resource table during networking or dessert/social time, appropriate information about approved products, disease areas, or clinical trials, and a limited number of representative seats when reviewed and disclosed.

Recruiting partners may participate through a separate career-opportunity table during networking or dessert/social time, without turning the educational discussion into a hiring pitch.

The fellow-led discussion should remain educational and trustworthy. Sponsor representatives should not direct the discussion, record private comments, receive individual prescribing insight, or receive identifiable attendee feedback. Recruiting conversations should stay separate from the core discussion.

Guardrails

Built to preserve trust with fellows and institutions.

Partner conversations should define the educational objective, permitted role, disclosure language, hospitality expectations, and reporting format before an event is opened for registration.

Review topics

  • Clinical independenceNo promises tied to prescribing behavior.
  • PrivacyNo identifiable patient information or private attendee lists.
  • Representative roleObserver or participant status defined in advance.
  • Recruiting roleCareer-opportunity tables kept separate from education.
  • HospitalityProfessional and modest event setting.
  • CMENo CME claim unless an accredited provider is involved.
  • ReportingAggregate event feedback, not individual prescribing insight.
