Scope And Review

Consulting should be defined before work begins.

Each engagement should clarify the clinical question, intended audience, permitted use, confidentiality requirements, deliverables, and whether the request involves patient-specific care or broader advisory work.

Scope checkpoints

  • Clinical scopeWhat question is being answered, and for whom?
  • LicensureDoes the work require a specific state or clinical relationship?
  • ConfidentialityWhat data, documents, or restrictions apply?
  • DeliverableCall, memo, review, advisory session, or program support.
  • ComplianceInstitutional, sponsor, and legal requirements reviewed as needed.
